Export today / week / all data as CSV or JSON. "More" has Copy Yesterday, meal templates, and quick weight log.

2 · Add Meal
Logging a meal — step by step
Click "+ Add Meal" or press Ctrl+N. The modal guides you through the entry.
1
Meal Category
Pick Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack. Affects the meal log grouping and the projection's category estimates.
2
Food Name + Autocomplete
Start typing any food. Matching items from your library appear instantly. Use ↑↓ arrows to navigate, Enter to pick.
3
Serving Amount
Enter how much you ate. Macros auto-scale from the food's base serving in the library.
4
Macro Fields
Auto-filled from your library. Edit manually if the prep was different. Calories, Protein, Fat, Carbs, Fiber.
5
Save to Library
Checked by default — saves this food for future autocomplete so you never have to re-enter macros.
6
Enter to Confirm
Press Enter from any field to submit. Or "Add Another" to log multiple items without closing the modal.

4 · Settings
Set your goals — simple or advanced
Fill in Personal Info first, then the app calculates everything else for you.
1
Profile Switcher
Each person (Alex, Mom, Dad) gets their own profile with independent goals and food logs.
2
Simple Mode
Toggle On for new users or parents. Hides Analysis, Projection, advanced macro settings, and Danger Zone — just the essentials remain.
3
Goal Dropdown
Pick your goal: Body Recomp, Gentle Loss (👴👵 best for older adults), Fat Loss, Maintenance, or Lean Bulk. Calorie target updates automatically.
4
Goal Planning (collapsible)
Click to expand — enter Body Fat %, Goal Weight, Start Date, and Timeline. Collapsed by default for new users.
5
Auto-Calculated Stats
BMI, BMR, TDEE, Calorie Target, Lean Body Mass, Fat to Lose, and Safe Timeline — all computed from your inputs.

Quick Reference
Which goal should I pick?
All goals auto-calculate calorie and macro targets from your stats.
⭐
Body Recomposition
Lose fat + build muscle simultaneously. Best for active people training 4–5×/week.
👴👵
Gentle Loss
−200 kcal/day · ~0.2 kg/week. Safe and sustainable, ideal for older adults or beginners.
🔥
Fat Loss
−500 kcal/day · ~0.5 kg/week. Faster results, requires consistent adherence.
⚖️
Maintenance
Eat at TDEE. Build habits before cutting or bulking.
💪
Lean Bulk
+300 kcal above TDEE. Slow muscle gain with minimal fat gain.

Pro Tips
Get more out of NutriLog
🌅
Pre-log meals the night before. The end-of-day projection uses your pre-logged meals directly instead of estimating from history — much more accurate.
⚖️
Weigh yourself consistently. Morning, after toilet, same conditions every time. 2–3 check-ins/week is enough to build a clean trend on the Projection page.
🥩
Hit protein first, fill the rest. Protein drives muscle retention. Once you hit your target, use remaining calories for carbs and fat based on preference.
📋
Save meal templates for repeated days. If you eat the same things on weekdays, save Monday's log as a template and apply it each morning in 2 seconds.
🏷️
Use variants for the same food, different prep. "Chicken Breast [grilled]" and "Chicken Breast [boiled]" have different macros. Save both — the tag appears in autocomplete so you always pick the right one.
🔬
Check Nutrition Insights weekly. The Analysis page auto-detects patterns — fiber consistently low, protein streak, calorie variability. Use it to course-correct before small issues become habits.
