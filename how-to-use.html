Getting Started
Set up in 3 minutes
Follow these steps when you open NutriLog for the first time.
1
Fill in Personal Info
Go to Settings → Personal Info. Enter your age, height, weight, gender, and activity level. The app will automatically calculate your TDEE and set your calorie target.
💡 You don't need to enter body fat % right away — leave it at the default 16% and update it later.
2
Choose Your Goal
In Settings → Personal Info → Goal, pick what you're working toward. Each option adjusts your calorie target automatically. See goal descriptions below.
👴👵 For older users or those who want slow, safe weight loss — choose Gentle Loss (−200 kcal).
3
Log Your First Meal
Click + Add Meal (top right). Type a food name — if it's in your library, it autocompletes. Enter the serving amount and click Add Meal (or press Enter).
💡 The app saves foods to your personal library automatically so future logging is faster.
4
Check Your Macro Rings
Back on the Today page, the four rings show Protein, Fat, Fiber, and Carbs as a percentage of your daily target. The calorie bar shows total calories vs your target range.
💡 Click the Summary button next to the calorie bar to see a projected end-of-day total based on your history.
5
Log Your Weight (Optional)
Click the ⚖️ weight widget on the Today page to record your current weight. This feeds into the Projection page's actual weight trend chart and the body composition model.
💡 Weigh yourself first thing in the morning, after using the toilet, for the most consistent readings.
Goals
Which goal should I pick?
All goals auto-calculate your calorie and macro targets based on your personal stats.
⭐
Body Recomposition
Lose fat + build muscle simultaneously. Best for active people who train regularly. Slow scale change, big body composition change.
👴👵
Gentle Loss
−200 kcal/day. About 0.2 kg/week loss. Safe and sustainable, especially for older adults or those new to tracking.
🔥
Fat Loss
−500 kcal/day. About 0.5 kg/week loss. Faster results but requires consistent adherence and higher protein intake.
⚖️
Maintenance
Eat at TDEE. No intentional weight change — useful for building healthy habits before cutting or bulking.
💪
Lean Bulk
+300 kcal/day above TDEE. Slow muscle gain with minimal fat. Best paired with a structured training programme.
🌱
Lifetime Mode (in Goal Planning) sets a tiny −100 kcal deficit that runs indefinitely — ideal if you've reached your goal weight and want gentle recomposition without a set end date.
Daily Logging
Logging meals quickly
There are four ways to add food to your log.

📝 Manual Entry

  • Click + Add Meal → type the food name. If it's in your library, it will autocomplete. Pick a match with ↑↓ + Enter.
  • Enter serving amount and press Enter to confirm.
  • New foods are automatically saved to your library for next time.
  • Use the category buttons (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Snack) to organise your log.

📋 Paste from Excel

  • Click Paste tab-separated data instead inside the Add Meal modal.
  • Copy rows from your Excel tracker (cols Q→AA from PART 3, or the full sheet).
  • The app auto-detects the format and skips TOTALS / TARGETS / blank rows.
  • Simple format also works: Food · Serving · Unit · kcal · Protein · Fat · Carbs · Fiber

⏪ Copy Yesterday

  • Click … More in the top bar → Copy Yesterday.
  • All of yesterday's meals are appended to today's log.
  • Also works from History — open any past day and use the copy action.

🍽️ Meal Templates

  • Click Show Templates on the Today page to see saved day templates.
  • Save any day as a template via the History page actions.
  • Apply a template to add all its meals to today with one click.
Food Database
Managing your food library
The Food Database stores all your foods with their macros for fast autocomplete when logging.
✏️
Add or Edit a Food
Click + Add Food in the Food Database page. Enter the food name, optional Variant (e.g. "grilled", "homemade"), default serving, unit, and all macros. The variant tag appears in the table and in autocomplete suggestions.
💡 Use variants to store the same food with different preps — e.g. "Chicken Breast [grilled]" vs "Chicken Breast [boiled]".
🔍
Filter by Macros
Use the macro filter bar above the food list to narrow down results: Protein ≥, Kcal ≤, Carbs ≤, Fat ≤, Fiber ≥. Combine with the search box for precise lookups.
📤
Export / Import
Use the Export button to download your food library as a TSV file. Import new foods by clicking Import and pasting TSV data or uploading a JSON export.
🔨
Recipe Builder
Click Build Recipe to create a composite food from ingredients. Enter ingredient names (from your library), amounts, and number of servings. The recipe is saved as a single food item in your database.
Analysis & Projection
Understanding your data
The Analysis and Projection pages turn your logged data into actionable insights.

📊 Analysis Page

  • Heatmap — one year of daily calorie data at a glance. Darker = more calories.
  • 12-Month Chart — average daily calories per month vs your target.
  • Achievements — unlock badges for streaks, milestones, and consistency.
  • Personal Records — best single-day stats across all history.
  • Nutrition Insights — automatic pattern detection (fiber streaks, calorie adherence, protein streaks, variability).

📈 Projection Page

  • Projection Model — week-by-week fat loss and lean mass gain forecast based on your deficit, protein, and training.
  • Weight Trend Chart — plots your actual check-in weights over time with a goal weight reference line.
  • Timeline Progress — shows how far through your plan you are, based on Plan Start Date in settings.
  • Projection Summary — plain-English summary of what the model predicts.

Profiles
Multiple users on one app
Each profile has its own goals, settings, and meal log — stored separately.
➕
Add a Profile
Go to Settings → Profiles → Add Profile. Type a name (e.g. "Mom", "Dad") and press Enter or click Add. Switch between profiles by clicking the name pill in the sidebar.
💡 Each profile is completely independent — different calorie targets, different food logs, different goals.
🧓
Simple Mode for Parents
For family members who find the app overwhelming, go to their profile's Settings → Appearance → Simple Mode → On. This hides advanced views (Analysis, Projection) and advanced settings, leaving only the essentials.
💡 Simple mode is saved per-device. The user just sees Today, History, Food Database, and Settings with a clean, minimal layout.

Pro Tips
Get the most out of NutriLog
Habits and features that make a real difference.
🌅
Pre-log your meals the night before. Log tomorrow's planned meals tonight. The end-of-day projection will use your pre-logged meals directly instead of estimating from history — giving you a more accurate picture.
⚖️
Weigh yourself consistently. Same time, same conditions (morning, after toilet, before eating). Even 2–3 check-ins per week gives the Projection page enough data to draw your actual weight trend.
🥩
Hit protein first, then fill the rest. Protein is the most important macro for body recomposition. Once you hit your protein target, use remaining calories for carbs and fat based on preference.
📋
Use meal templates for repeated days. If you eat the same things most weekdays, save Monday's log as a template and apply it each morning. It takes 2 seconds instead of re-entering every item.
🔬
Check Nutrition Insights weekly. The Analysis page automatically detects patterns — fiber consistently low, protein streak on a roll, calorie variability too high. Use it to course-correct before small issues become habits.
